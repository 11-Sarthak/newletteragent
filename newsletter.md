#### Research Papers Summary
* **Title**: AI prediction leads people to forgo guaranteed rewards
* **Authors**: Aoi Naito, Hirokazu Shirado
* **Published**: March 30, 2026
* **Summary**: This research paper explores how AI can change how people decide, using a behavioral implementation of the classic Newcomb's paradox in 1,305 participants. The results show that belief in predictive authority can lead individuals to constrain decision-making, forgoing a guaranteed reward, with significant implications for AI development and deployment.
